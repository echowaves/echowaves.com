AI-tells audit

review-ai-tells.md · hostile "still slop?" pass

The Blueprint direction is past the slop threshold in structure — a career schematic with dimensioned annotations isn't prompt-default, and the Do's/Don'ts explicitly kill the worst tells. But three residual risks keep it from being unmistakably human: uniform sheet composition, the metric strip as stat-pill silhouette, and voice rules that are negative rather than personal. As specified: will read as a very good template, not yet as Dmitry's hand.

high

Uniform region composition — implementer defaults will reintroduce band-stacking

DESIGN.md § Layout & Spacing · EXPERIENCE.md § IA
Every region gets identical gutter/gap/hairline. Real engineering sheets are asymmetric: title blocks in corners, revision stamps, field notes pinned where they don't fit. Nothing mandates a single layout break.
Fix: mandate ≥2 deliberate layout breaks per sheet + one stamp/revision artifact; name them in DESIGN.md.
high

Metric strip = stat pills wearing a trench coat

direction-04 mock bottom strip · DESIGN.md § Components
Five evenly-spaced equal-weight cells with no anchor is the classic AI hero-stat row. Unit+timeframe binding is necessary but not sufficient.
Fix: strip becomes a legend pointing at schematic rows; forbid equal weight — one designated primary reading per view.
medium

Annotation layer is descopable — and without it, this is a generic dark-cyan dev site

DESIGN.md § Components
Grid texture + hairlines alone don't separate from the dev-portfolio default; callouts are the differentiator and also the expensive part.
Fix: make annotations load-bearing in contract ("a region without annotations is a spec violation").
medium

Terminal motif is one wink deep; the old easter egg is unprotected

EXPERIENCE.md § IA · memlog (upside-down line)
~/echowaves, $ npm install the-future, whoami are the expected geek props a model reaches for first. The decodable upside-down line was the most human artifact on the current site and nothing carries it forward.
Fix: require ≥1 decodable personal artifact per sheet; revive the upside-down line's spirit.
medium

Voice rules are negative only — absence-of-bad isn't presence-of-voice

EXPERIENCE.md § Voice and Tone
A model can follow every "don't" and still write flat copy.
Fix: add 3–4 positive signature patterns (value · unit · context; one deadpan annotation note per region; we/they for team outcomes, I only where personally true).
low

No imperfection budget

DESIGN.md Do's
Hyper-clean-everything is its own AI tell. One sanctioned rough edge (rotated stamp, hand correction line, revision row) signals a human made choices.
Fix: allow exactly one imperfection per region in Do's.
low

Thesis and CTA strings not committed literally

EXPERIENCE.md § IA
"AI-native engineering transformation, measured." will be paraphrased into mush by implementers/AIs.
Fix: pin literal strings for title block + contact CTA in the spines.
