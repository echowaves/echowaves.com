Method

  1. Measure what you have. Cycle time, lead time, release cadence, defect rate, and how long a change waits before a human trusts it. Most organizations cannot answer these on demand, which is the first finding.
  2. Build one thing that ships. A single gate in the path your teams already use, with agent output held to the same standard as human output. One, not nine — a gate nobody uses is a document.
  3. Get it adopted. The engineering change is small; the organizational change is not. Teams adopt what they helped design and what does not punish them, so both of those are engineered on purpose.
  4. Hand it over. Your engineers reproduce the measurement without me and the gate survives a release cycle. If it cannot, it was never yours.

What I do now

Help engineering organizations get durable gains from agentic software engineering. Not a tool rollout and not a strategy deck: the part where humans change how they build.
